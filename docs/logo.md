**promtp**
A modern, clean vector logo for a brand named "BookMe", an app to discover local experiences and book them instantly.

Icon: A friendly, playful ticket character made of a vibrant orange (#FF6B00) ticket shape tilted slightly. The ticket features a simple white smiling face, along with cute, thin, minimalist stick-like arms and legs waving cheerfully. Flat vector style with smooth, soft curves. No background shapes, rings, frames, or location pins behind or around it.

Wordmark: Below the icon, "BookMe" in a clean, bold, rounded sans-serif font, with "Book" in dark charcoal/black (#111111) and "Me" in vibrant orange (#FF6B00), with balanced letter spacing.

Tagline: Underneath, "LOCAL EXPERIENCES & INSTANT BOOKING" in small, clean, widely spaced uppercase black letters.

Style: Flat vector illustration, solid colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects, high contrast, centered composition, generous white space. Clean off-white background (#FAF8F5). Friendly, memorable, startup branding character.
